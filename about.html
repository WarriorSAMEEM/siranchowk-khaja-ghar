About Siranchowk Khaja Ghar

Simple food, clean environment, and respectful service in the heart of Parsa, Chitwan.

Local Food Spot

Fresh food made for daily life

Siranchowk Khaja Ghar (सिरानचोक खाजा घर) was established to give people in Khairahani and Parsa a reliable, welcoming spot for their everyday meals and tea breaks.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick breakfast of hot paratha and milk tea, enjoying chicken or buff momo with friends in the afternoon, or picking up fried chicken and chowmein for dinner, we prepare every dish with care.

Our motto — "Your Satisfaction Is Our Satisfaction" — guides everything we do, from keeping our kitchen clean to serving food with a smile.
